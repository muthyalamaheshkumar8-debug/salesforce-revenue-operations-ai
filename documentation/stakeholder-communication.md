# Sales / CSM / Marketing communication practice
The **Team handoff** view creates separate audience-specific review briefs from the full reviewed sample, with source period, value definition, conversion denominator, missing-account count and a decision request. Export a draft and record a real decision after an actual review. The app does not send emails, Slack messages or invitations.

Weekly review agenda: confirm scope and freshness; reconcile totals; examine exceptions; assign owners and due dates; confirm Sales/CSM handoff; agree Marketing attribution scope; log decision and next review. Keep attendee names and private links outside the public portfolio.

Evidence template: actual meeting date, consenting attendees and roles, reviewed report version, decisions, owners, due dates, follow-up confirmation. A document template or sample brief is not proof of stakeholder communication.
